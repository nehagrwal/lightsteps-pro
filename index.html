Risks and Limitations

The discrepancy between the evidence for individual ingredients and the evidence for the finished product is one significant drawback. Although studies on hibiscus, bromelain, beetroot root, and other plant substances may provide helpful context, they do not demonstrate that LightSteps Pro yields the same outcomes. The biological effects might differ greatly depending on the dosage, formulation, extraction techniques, and constituent combinations.

Additionally, plant-based compounds may interact with drugs or have adverse consequences. Products made from beetroot may modify the colour of urine or faeces as well as the exposure to nitrates. Hibiscus may have an impact on blood pressure, while pineapple extracts containing bromelain may trigger allergic reactions or digestive issues in those who are vulnerable. Instead of assuming natural ingredients are risk-free, people who take medications that impact blood pressure, blood coagulation, or other cardiovascular functions should speak with a certified healthcare provider before using these supplements.

LightSteps Pro should not be used in place of an immediate assessment of symptoms that could be dangerous.
